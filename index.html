Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about QR codes and Artvizual’s services.

Are the QR codes generated here really free?

Yes. All standard QR code generators on this page are free to use and create utility QR codes you can download as PNG files. Artvizual’s premium services only apply if you choose to commission custom artistic QR designs.

Can I use these QR codes for commercial projects?

You can typically use the generated QR codes in commercial contexts such as menus, posters, flyers or product packaging. You are responsible for the content you link to and for complying with any platform or local regulations.

How do I make sure my QR code is scannable?

Use strong contrast between foreground and background, avoid making the QR too small, choose higher error correction for print, and always test with multiple phones and lighting conditions before printing thousands of copies.

What’s the difference between free and premium QR codes?

Free tools on this page output standard QR codes with basic visual customization. Artvizual’s premium services layer custom illustration, branding and sometimes AI‑assisted artwork around the QR while preserving scan reliability.
